## What They All Have in Common

### Theme & Color
- Dark/black backgrounds dominate — signals nightlife culture
- One accent color per brand (purple, teal, blue) for instant recognition
- High contrast white text on dark backgrounds
- Gradients for depth without complexity

### Typography
- Sans-serif only — no decorative fonts
- Headlines range from 36px to 144px — big = confident
- Uppercase navigation text
- Generous whitespace

### Hero Section
- Full-screen video backgrounds (autoplay, muted, looping) with dark overlay
- Short powerful taglines — one sentence max
- Immediate CTA button visible without scrolling

### Navigation
- 3-5 items max: Roster, About, Book/Contact
- Sticky/fixed headers, transparent over hero
- Hamburger on mobile with full-screen overlay

### Artist Roster (The Core Product)
- Grid layout, 3-5 columns on desktop
- Square or portrait thumbnails with name overlay
- Hover reveals more info (bio, genre, booking button)
- Filtering: alphabetical A-Z and/or genre categories
- Some have shortlist/favorites functionality (MN2S)
- Modal popups vs dedicated artist pages — both work

### Booking Flow
- Prominent "Book" or "Booking Request" in main nav — never buried
- Progressive disclosure forms (multi-step) > single long forms
- Fields: event type, date, location, budget, artist interest
- AJAX submission (no page reload)

### Animations
- Scroll-triggered fade/slide-ins
- Smooth cubic-bezier easing
- Parallax on section backgrounds
- Ken Burns (slow zoom) on hero images
- Some use WebGL/shaders for generative backgrounds

### Trust & Credibility
- Physical office addresses in footer
- Team/agents page with photos
- "Established [year]" timestamps
- Award mentions, press logos
- Case studies or partnership showcases

## Ideas That Could Differentiate Pro Spin

### From the research
- **Generative art background** (like Octopus Agents) — unique, memorable, on-brand
- **"Shows Near You"** geolocation feature (like Ground Control) — useful if roster grows
- **Embedded music player** (like Inner Circle) — let visitors hear what our DJs sound like
- **AI-powered talent search** (like MN2S) — "describe your event and we'll match you"
- **Shortlist functionality** — let event planners save DJs they're interested in

### Original ideas
- **Video reels per DJ** — short 15-30s clips of live sets, not just photos
- **"Build Your Event" tool** — interactive: pick event type, vibe, genre → get DJ recommendations
- **Culture blog / editorial** — positions Pro Spin as tastemakers, not just a booking service
- **Testimonial carousel** — quotes from brand managers, sports orgs
- **Live availability calendar** — show which DJs are available for specific dates
- **Genre mood board** — visual/audio previews of what each genre feels like at an event

## Visual Direction Options

### Option A: Dark + Purple (current)
Black background, purple accent. Aligns with Active Talent Agency. Nightclub energy. Most common in the space.

### Option B: Dark + Gold/Amber
Black background, gold accent. Signals premium/luxury. Differentiates from the purple/teal crowd. Aligns with the "premium but not exclusive" tone.

### Option C: Dark + Generative Art
Black background with animated gradient blobs or shader effects. Like Octopus Agents. Most visually distinctive. Could cycle through warm colors (amber, coral, peach).

### Option D: Minimal Black & White
Pure black and white, photography-driven. Like Bullitt Agency. Lets the DJ imagery do all the talking. Cleanest, hardest to pull off without great photos.
