## 3. Featured Case Studies

### Aritzia: High-Volume Logistics Management

- **The Challenge:** Programming 84+ hours of continuous, brand-aligned music for the massive Aritzia Warehouse Sale.
- **The Pro Spin Solution:** We managed the entire DJ rotation for 7 days (12 hours daily). Our focus was on "The Smooth Switchover" — ensuring zero silence between sets and a vibe that evolved from morning shopping flow to peak afternoon energy.
- **The Result:** Seamless technical execution and a consistent, high-energy atmosphere for one of Canada's largest retail events.

### Lululemon: Global Curation at BNP Paribas Open

- **The Challenge:** Creating a musical identity for the "Lululemon Combine" in California that resonated with an international elite athletic crowd.
- **The Pro Spin Solution:** We curated and created the full sonic identity and playlists for the activation, bridging the gap between Lululemon's Vancouver roots and the global stage of Indian Wells.
- **The Result:** A sophisticated, high-performance atmosphere that kept athletes and spectators engaged throughout the tournament.

### The "Big Four" Sport Activations (Nike, Adidas, Puma, JD Sports)

- **The Challenge:** Delivering specific, trend-forward energy for the world's leading athletic brands.
- **The Pro Spin Solution:** We provided specialized DJ services for Nike, Adidas, and Puma product launches and store activations. For JD Sports, we leaned into the "Street-to-Sport" crossover — keeping the music current, family-friendly, and high-energy.
- **The Result:** Reliable, brand-standard execution that maintained momentum and shopper retention for peak retail hours.

**The Pro Spin Solution:** We are the bridge. We specialize in World Music — mixing global sounds that make international fans feel at home, while staying rooted in Vancouver's local scene.
